# Apple Inc. revenue review

FY2023–FY2025. Annual reported net sales. Golla Varshith.

FY2025 revenue was **$416.2B**, up **6.4%** from FY2024.

- Services added $13.0B, accounting for 51.7% of the net increase.
- Services represented 26.2% of revenue, a +1.6 percentage-point change from FY2024. iPhone remained the largest category at 50.4%.
- Greater China YoY: -3.8%. Wearables, Home and Accessories YoY: -3.6%.

## Method

YoY = current revenue / prior revenue − 1. Revenue share = category or regional revenue / total sales. Share of net growth = category or regional FY2025 change / consolidated FY2025 change. Product and regional tables are two views of the same revenue; they must not be added together.

Each extract reconciles independently to reported consolidated revenue in all three years. FY2023 contains 53 weeks, compared with 52 weeks in FY2024 and FY2025. FY2023 YoY is unavailable because FY2022 is outside this extract. No adjustment has been made for the extra week, currency, inflation or product mix.

These figures describe revenue, not customer retention, unit sales or profitability. The extracts are a manually collected historical snapshot; running this script does not fetch new filings.
